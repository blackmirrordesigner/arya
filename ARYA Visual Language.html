00

The principle

The product is the only hero. The language exists to disappear behind it.

ARYA preserves what nature already perfected. The visual language does the same — it adds nothing the oil did not earn. Bright, warm, architectural daylight is the constant; everything else is restraint.

This is not a blend of three styles. It is one identity built from a clear hierarchy: Daylight is who ARYA is, Material is how the product is examined, Editorial is how the words are read. The user should feel clarity — never styling.

“If a decision does not make the product more honest, it is removed.”

01

The hierarchy

Three worlds, one brand. They are not equal — they are weighted.

70
Daylight · Foundation

The emotional identity. Architectural, bright, warm white, vast negative space. The bottle is always placed, never staged. When in doubt, this wins.

20
Material

Only where the physical product becomes the focus — macro, viscosity, refraction, glass. Never decoration. Never dominant.

10
Editorial

Only to improve reading — typography, rhythm, spacing, captions, margins. Borrowed quietly; never a magazine.

Read the proportions literally. Most of any ARYA page is Daylight — calm warm-white space holding a single placed object. Material appears as a focused interruption when the user wants to examine the oil. Editorial is invisible infrastructure: the grid and type that make everything legible. Mixed correctly, the three never read as three.

Space & grid

Whitespace is not empty. It is the courtyard the object stands in.

Object placed on the grid — held by space, not centered by default

Layout follows a 12-column architectural grid with structural margins — the proportions of a building section, not a storefront. Composition is allowed to be asymmetric: an object placed against generous void reads as confidence. Alignment is strict; emptiness is intentional.

05

Photography

Photography carries all the emotion. It is photographed, never rendered.

Daylight is the default mode: one subject, real architectural light, enormous warm-white space. Material mode is the close-up — used only when the user wants to examine the oil itself: viscosity, the meniscus, light bending through heavy glass. The two share the same warm light, so a page never feels like two shoots.

Always

Warm-white ground · single soft daylight source · the product physically real · tiny imperfections kept · generous space around the subject.

Never

Black or dramatic backgrounds · studio spotlights · 3D renders · props, hands, scenes · retouched-to-perfection surfaces · cinematic atmosphere.

06

Motion

Motion behaves like daylight — it appears, it does not perform.

Duration
1100ms · slow
Long enough to feel like light shifting, never a transition you notice.
Easing
cubic-bezier(.2,.6,.2,1)
A soft settle. No overshoot, no spring, no bounce.
Properties
opacity · 13px drift
Fade and a small rise only. Nothing scales, pops, or slides far.

Things appear naturally, as if the light reached them. Reveals trigger once on scroll and never repeat. There are no carousels, parallax stunts, or attention-seeking loops. The only living motion belongs to the product itself — a reflection shifting, the oil settling — and even that is almost invisible.

07

Voice

State the truth plainly. Restraint is the tone.

We say

“Cold-pressed linseed. One source. Nothing added.”

Few words. Concrete. The product does the convincing. Facts over adjectives.

We never say

“Unlock radiant wellness with nature's golden elixir, ethically crafted for your best self.”

No wellness language, no superlatives, no Ayurveda or luxury clichés, no selling.

The decision filter

Does this make the product feel more honest?

Run every choice — every element, word, image, and motion — through this one question. If the answer is no, remove it. That is the whole language.
